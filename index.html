மெய் எழுத்துகள் · Consonants

18 consonants, each written with a dot on top (புள்ளி). Grouped by how they sound.

In school a pure consonant is usually read with a short இ before it — க் is said “ik”, ங் “ing”. This page plays it the same way so the sound is clear.

ஆய்த எழுத்து · The special letter

One letter, also called அஃகேனம் or முப்புள்ளி (three dots).

ஃ always comes between a short vowel and a வல்லினம் letter, as in எஃகு (steel) and அஃது (that). Its sound is a soft breathy “kh”.
