Letter Recorder

Record the correct sound of each Tamil letter. The Learn Tamil page then plays your voice instead of the device voice.
How to use
  1. Use a quiet room. Allow the microphone when the browser asks.
  2. Pick a letter, press Record (or Space), say the letter once, press again to stop. Silence at the start and end is trimmed automatically.
  3. Listen (P). If it is right, go to the next letter (→). Progress is saved in this browser.
  4. Press Download audio.zip and unzip it into the learntamil/audio/ folder, next to index.html. You can do this part-way and again later.
