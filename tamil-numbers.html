❓Questions about numbers 1–10 in Tamil

How do you count from 1 to 10 in Tamil?

1 ஒன்று (onru), 2 இரண்டு (irandu), 3 மூன்று (moonru), 4 நான்கு (naangu), 5 ஐந்து (ainthu), 6 ஆறு (aaru), 7 ஏழு (ezhu), 8 எட்டு (ettu), 9 ஒன்பது (onbathu), 10 பத்து (pathu).

How are numbers written in Tamil script?

Tamil has its own numerals: ௧ (1), ௨ (2), ௩ (3), ௪ (4), ௫ (5), ௬ (6), ௭ (7), ௮ (8), ௯ (9) and ௰ (10). Today the digits 1, 2, 3 … are used in everyday writing; Tamil numerals are seen in older texts, calendars and formal uses.
