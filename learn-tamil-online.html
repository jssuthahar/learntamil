You can learn Tamil online for free on Learn Tamil: start by hearing the 12 vowels and 18 consonants, join them into uyirmei letters, then read picture words, phrases and short stories — about 10 to 15 minutes a day. Everything below is free, needs no sign-up and works on phones, tablets and computers.

🪜The learning path, step by step

  1. Hear the letters. Open the learning app and tap any letter to hear it. Start with the 12 vowels (உயிர்).
  2. Learn the consonants. Learn the 18 consonants (மெய்) in their three groups. The pronunciation guide explains the sounds English does not have, such as ழ.
  3. Join them into uyirmei letters. A consonant plus a vowel makes a new letter: க் + ஆ = கா. Learn one row of the uyirmei chart at a time.
  4. Write the letters. Trace letters on screen (Games → Trace), then on paper with the free tracing worksheets.
  5. Read your first words. Read two-letter picture words, then words by topic: numbers, colours, animals and more.
  6. Say simple phrases. Use everyday Tamil phrases such as வணக்கம் (hello) and நன்றி (thank you) at home.
  7. Read stories, rhymes and Thirukkural. Move on to stories, rhymes, Aathichoodi and Thirukkural.
  8. Test yourself. Take the 10 certificate levels to check what you have learned.

🔤Tamil script in one minute

Tamil has 247 letters: 12 vowels (உயிர்), 18 consonants (மெய்), 1 special letter ஃ and 216 consonant-vowel letters (உயிர்மெய், 18 × 12). That sounds like a lot, but the 216 follow a pattern: each consonant takes the same vowel sign, so once you know the 30 main letters and the 12 signs, you can read them all. ❓Questions about learning Tamil online

Can I learn Tamil online for free?

Yes. Learn Tamil (learntamil.msdevbuild.com) is completely free with no sign-up. It teaches all 247 Tamil letters with sound, picture words, reading practice, read-aloud stories, rhymes, Thirukkural, 14 games, printable worksheets and certificate tests, on any phone, tablet or computer.

Can adults and beginners use Learn Tamil?

Yes. Learn Tamil is made for children aged 3 to 12, but the path is the same for any beginner: hear the letters, learn the 12 vowels and 18 consonants, then the 216 uyirmei letters, then read words and short stories. Adults often use it alongside their children.

What is the best way to start learning Tamil?

Start with the sounds. Learn the 12 vowels (உயிர், uyir) and the 18 consonants (மெய், mei) first, then join them into uyirmei letters such as க் + ஆ = கா. Practise about 10 to 15 minutes every day, listen to Tamil often, and read simple picture words as soon as you know a few letters.

Can I learn Tamil from English?

Yes. Every letter on Learn Tamil has a romanised sound (for example அ = a, கா = kaa) and an English hint, and every word and story line has an English meaning, so English speakers can follow without knowing any Tamil.

Does Learn Tamil work on a phone and offline?

Yes. It works in any modern browser and can be installed as an app on Android, iPhone, iPad and computers. After the first visit, the letters, games, stories and guides work without internet.
