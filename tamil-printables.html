❓Questions about the printables

Are the Tamil worksheets free to print?

Yes. All charts and tracing worksheets on this page are free for home and classroom use. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like.

What paper size are the printables?

They are made for A4 paper. On US Letter paper, choose “Fit to page” in the print settings.

How should a child use the tracing worksheets?

Look at the blue letter, say its sound, then trace the grey outline letters from left to right. One row a day is plenty for young children.
