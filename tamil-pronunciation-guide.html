Most Tamil sounds are easy for English speakers; the ones that need practice are the special ழ (zha), the three “l” sounds ல ள ழ, the three “n” sounds ந ன ண, the two “r” sounds ர ற, and the difference between short and long vowels. This guide explains each one with an example word. To hear them, tap the letters in the learning app.

👂Three kinds of “n”: ந ன ண

ந and ன sound almost the same; the difference is mainly where they are written. ண is said with the tongue curled back.

LetterSoundHow to say itExample
ந்n“n”, tongue at the teeth — mostly at the start of a word or before தபந்து — ball
ன்ṉ“n”, tongue at the gum — mostly in the middle or end of a wordமீன் — fish
ண்ṇ“n” with the tongue curled backகண் — eye

👂Three kinds of “l”: ல ள ழ

ல is like English “l”. For ள curl the tongue back and touch the roof of the mouth. For ழ curl it back further without touching, and let the sound flow — it is the sound in தமிழ் (Tamizh).

LetterSoundHow to say itExample
ல்l“l” as in “love”பல் — tooth
ள்ḷ“l” with the tongue curled backதேள் — scorpion
ழ்zhspecial to Tamil — tongue curled far back, between “r” and “l”தமிழ் — Tamil

👂Two kinds of “r”: ர ற

ர is a light tap of the tongue. ற is stronger and rolled. When it is doubled, ற்ற sounds close to “tr”, as in காற்று (kaatru, wind).

LetterSoundHow to say itExample
ர்rsoft tapped “r”நீர் — water
ற்ṟstrong trilled “r”காற்று — wind

👂Two kinds of “t”: த ட

For த the tongue touches the back of the upper teeth (a soft t). For ட the tongue curls back (a hard t).

LetterSoundHow to say itExample
த்thsoft “t”, tongue touching the teethபுத்தகம் — book
ட்ṭhard “t”, tongue curled backபட்டம் — kite

🌱Short and long vowels

Five Tamil vowels come in short (குறில்) and long (நெடில்) pairs. A long vowel is held about twice as long.

ShortSoundLongSound
அa — “a” as in “about”ஆā — “aa” as in “father”
இi — “i” as in “sit”ஈī — “ee” as in “see”
உu — “u” as in “put”ஊū — “oo” as in “food”
எe — “e” as in “egg”ஏē — long “e”, like “a” in “gate”
ஒo — short “o”, as in “omit”ஓō — long “o”, as in “open”

🔁Letters that soften inside a word

Some hard letters are said more softly between vowels or after a nasal sound. For example க in புத்தகம் (book) sounds like “g” — puththagam — and த in பந்து (ball) sounds like “dh” — pandhu. ச can be “ch” or “s”. You will pick these up naturally by listening to words.

❓Questions about Tamil pronunciation

How do you pronounce ழ (zha) in Tamil?

To say ழ (zha), curl the tip of your tongue far back towards the roof of your mouth without touching it, and let the sound flow. It sounds between “r” and “l”. It is the last sound of தமிழ் (Tamizh), the name of the language, and is often written “zh” in English.

What is the difference between ல, ள and ழ?

ல (la) is like the English “l”, with the tongue just behind the upper teeth. ள (ḷa) is said with the tongue curled back so it touches the roof of the mouth. ழ (zha) is said with the tongue curled back even further, without touching. Practise them in pairs: பல் (tooth), தேள் (scorpion), தமிழ் (Tamil).

What is the difference between ர and ற?

ர (ra) is a soft, quick tap of the tongue, as in நீர் (water). ற (ṟa) is a stronger, rolled r, as in காற்று (wind). When ற is doubled (ற்ற) it sounds close to “tr”.

Why are there three n letters in Tamil?

Tamil has ந, ன and ண. ந is usually written at the start of a word or before த (பந்து, ball), ன in the middle or at the end (மீன், fish), and ண is said with the tongue curled back (கண், eye). ந and ன sound almost the same.

Do short and long vowels change the meaning in Tamil?

Yes. Tamil has five short vowels (அ இ உ எ ஒ) and their long partners (ஆ ஈ ஊ ஏ ஓ). Holding a vowel longer can make a different word, so practise each short–long pair together.
