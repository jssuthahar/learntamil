Share Learn Tamil – Videos, Posts and QR Codes

Updated 14 November 2026 · By Suthahar Jegatheesan

🦜Share with friends!

You can download and share these free Learn Tamil videos, Instagram posts and QR codes with any Tamil family, teacher or community group. Each video has a QR code that opens learntamil.msdevbuild.com, where children aged 3–12 learn all 247 Tamil letters with sound, stories, rhymes and 14 games. Every file is free to share. Please keep it as it is.

  • 🎬6 short videosVertical 1080×1920, for Reels, Shorts and WhatsApp Status
  • 🖼️12 posts & storiesInstagram-ready images
  • 📱QR codesScan to open the website or the install page
  • 💛Free to shareUse them to tell others about Learn Tamil

🎬Short videos with voice and QR code

Each video is a vertical MP4 with a Tamil voice, an English voice, music and a “Scan to open” QR code. Tap ⬇ to save it, then post it as an Instagram Reel, a YouTube Short or a WhatsApp Status. A caption you can copy is under each video.

🖼️Instagram posts

Square-ish 1080×1350 images. Post the five “Carousel” images together, in order, as one swipeable post.

📲Stories and partner posts

Stories are 1080×1920. Add Instagram’s link sticker over the button at the bottom. The two “advertise” images are for businesses that want to sponsor our guide pages.

📱QR codes

Print a QR code on a school newsletter, a class notice board or a Pongal event poster. For charts and worksheets to print, see the free Tamil printables.

🤝How to share

  • WhatsApp: send a video to your family or school group with the link learntamil.msdevbuild.com.
  • Instagram: post a video as a Reel and keep “Original audio” on so the voice stays. Tag our Instagram page so we can say thank you.
  • Tamil schools and sangams: show a video at the start of class, or put the QR code in your newsletter.
  • Please keep the files as they are (Tamil text, QR code and website address), and don’t add children’s photos or names to them.

❓Questions about sharing

Can I share Learn Tamil videos and posters?

Yes. Parents, teachers, Tamil schools and community groups can download the Learn Tamil videos, Instagram posts, stories and QR codes on this page and share them for free on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, in newsletters or on notice boards. Please keep them as they are and link to learntamil.msdevbuild.com.

Where does the QR code go?

The QR code in the videos and on this page opens the Learn Tamil website, learntamil.msdevbuild.com, where children aged 3–12 can learn all 247 Tamil letters with sound, stories, rhymes and games. It is free and needs no sign-up.

Who made the voices and music in the videos?

The Tamil and English voices are computer voices, and the music and sound effects were made for Learn Tamil, so the videos are free to share. If you hear a word that sounds wrong, please report it so we can fix it.

Can I put the Learn Tamil QR code on a school notice board?

Yes. Download the QR code from this page and print it on a poster or newsletter. Scanning it opens the free Learn Tamil website. Our free printable charts and worksheets are also on the Tamil printables page.
