Privacy & data policy

Updated 11 November 2026 · By Suthahar Jegatheesan

🦜No tracking — promise!
In short: Learn Tamil never sells personal information and never asks a child for it. The only personal details it keeps are certificate records that a parent or guardian gives on the website, with consent (the child’s name and the parent’s email and phone), and nobody can see them publicly. There are no accounts and no advertising scripts or ad tracking. Some parents’ guide pages show a simple sponsor box, never inside the children’s lessons or games. If you allow it, we count anonymous visits and game use with Google Analytics for Firebase, with all advertising features turned off. Progress and settings are saved only in your own browser.

Who runs this website

Learn Tamil (https://learntamil.msdevbuild.com/) is a free educational website made by Suthahar Jegatheesan under the MSDEVBUILD name. It is a personal, non-commercial project. Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page.

Information we collect

Almost no personal information. The site has no sign-up, no login and no comments. The one form that sends information is the certificate form on the website, filled in by a parent or guardian with their consent (see Certificate records). Apart from that, we do not ask for names, email addresses, ages, photos or any other personal details, and we do not run advertising scripts.

Sponsor boxes

Some guide pages for parents and teachers (and the parents’ area at the bottom of the home page) may show a small box marked Sponsored or Sample ad. It is plain text or an image with a link, chosen by us — not by an ad network. It loads no advertising script, sets no cookies, is not personalised or targeted, and sends nothing about you or your child to the sponsor. Nothing is sent until you tap the link, and then the sponsor’s own website and privacy policy apply. Sponsor boxes never appear inside the children’s lessons, games, stories or certificates. Businesses interested in sponsoring can read Advertise with us and our advertising policy.

Microphone (“Say it back”)

The 🎤 Say it button lets a child record themselves saying a letter or word and play it back. The recording is kept only in the browser’s memory, is never uploaded or saved, and is deleted when the window closes. The browser asks for permission first, and nothing is recorded until the child taps 🎤. There is no speech recognition.

Child profiles and Parent dashboard

Up to six children can each have their own progress on one device. A child’s first name (if you type one), the age group you choose (3–5, 6–8 or 9–12), tokens, badges, daily gifts, lessons done, Thirukkural couplets learned and bookmarked, stickers, minutes learned and the letters they find hard are saved only in this browser (localStorage) and are never sent anywhere. You can delete a child’s progress in the Parent dashboard, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Anonymous usage statistics (only if you allow them)

When you first visit, a notice asks whether we may count visits. Nothing is loaded or sent unless you tap “Allow”. If you allow it, the site uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which lessons and games are useful. It records pages visited and simple events such as “a game was started”, “a story was finished”, “a badge was earned” or “a certificate level was passed”, together with general technical details (approximate country, device type, browser). It stores a random visitor ID in first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*) so repeat visits can be counted. We have turned off Google signals, advertising cookies and ad personalisation, and we never send names, the certificate name or anything your child types. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy. If you tap “No thanks”, or change your mind later, the site works exactly the same. Your choice is saved in this browser (localStorage lt-consent).

Your choice is saved in this browser.

Certificate records (website only, with a parent’s consent)

On the website, before a certificate is made, a form asks a parent or guardian for the child’s name, their own email address and phone number (with country code), and to tick a box to agree. Nothing on the form is sent until you tick that box and tap the button.

  • What is stored: the child’s name, your email and phone, the certificate ID, the level or chapter passed, the date and time, that you agreed (and which version of the wording), and whether you later tapped a WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn or Instagram share button for that certificate.
  • Where: Cloud Firestore (Google Firebase, project learntamil-js). One record per certificate. The security rules let the website add a record once; nobody can read records from the website, so names, emails and phone numbers are never shown publicly, on the certificate or in share links. Only the site owner can see them, in the Firebase console.
  • Checking a certificate: a copy of only what is printed on the certificate (the child’s name, the level or chapter, the date and the certificate ID) is kept separately, so anyone who has a certificate ID can confirm it with 🔎 Check a certificate. The email and phone are never part of that copy and can’t be looked up. Certificates are never listed or searchable by name.
  • Why: to keep a record of each certificate, so we can confirm it if asked, and to tell you about new Learn Tamil levels and lessons. We never sell your details or share them with advertisers.
  • Your choices: to see, correct or delete your records, or to stop hearing from us, use the contact page. Your email and phone are also remembered in this browser (lt-parent-contact) so you are not asked again; clearing this site’s data removes them from the device.
  • Offline: the child always gets the certificate. If the record cannot be sent, it waits in this browser and is sent the next time the site is online.
  • Android app: the form does not appear and nothing is sent.

The Learn Tamil Android app

The Learn Tamil app on Android (Google Play or a test copy) shows this same website inside the phone’s Chrome engine. Because it is made for children, the app collects and sends nothing: there is no cookie notice and no Google Analytics, no “learning around the world” visit counter, no location lookup, no reminders or push token, no sponsor boxes and no donation links. Progress and settings are saved only on the phone, as listed below, and the 🎤 Say it microphone works the same way as on the website (never recorded to storage, never uploaded). The app remembers that it is the app only for the current session (sessionStorage lt-app). Uninstalling the app, or clearing Chrome’s data for this site, removes everything.

“Learning around the world” counter

The home page shows how many visits the site has had, how many people are learning right now, and the top countries and cities. To make this work, each visit adds one to a shared counter in Firebase Realtime Database (Google), and an open page keeps a temporary anonymous entry (a random ID, removed when the page is closed) so it can be counted as “learning right now”. No cookies are used for this.

Only if you allowed the cookie notice, the site also asks GeoJS for the approximate country and town of your internet connection, and adds one to that country and town on the map. Your IP address is sent to GeoJS for this lookup but is never stored by Learn Tamil; only the country and town name are kept, as part of the totals. If you tap “No thanks”, no place is looked up.

Reminders (push notifications)

You can choose to get occasional reminders, such as “Time to learn a new letter!”. They are off until you tap Turn on reminders and your browser asks for permission. When you turn them on, Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) gives this browser a random device token so a reminder can be delivered. The token is not linked to a name, email or any other personal detail, and is saved in this browser (localStorage lt-push). Turning reminders off deletes the token. You can also block notifications for this site in your browser settings at any time.

Microphone

Only the optional Letter Recorder page uses the microphone, and only after you allow it and press Record. Recordings stay in your browser. They are never uploaded by this website; they leave your device only if you choose to download the zip file yourself.

Services provided by others

Like most websites, Learn Tamil relies on a few outside services. They may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser contacts them:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (project learntamil-js) receives the anonymous usage statistics described above, only if you allow them.
  • Share buttons (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Telegram, email) are plain links: no script or tracking button from those sites loads on our pages. Nothing is sent until you tap one, and then only the page link and title (or the Kural you chose) go to the site you picked, under that site’s own privacy policy. We never include your child’s name or progress. After a certificate test, the share buttons sit behind a press-and-hold check for grown-ups. Their message and link never contain the child’s name. The name is only inside the certificate image, which leaves your device only if you choose to share or save it.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google Firebase) keeps the certificate records a parent or guardian chooses to give (see Certificate records).
  • Firebase Realtime Database keeps the anonymous visit totals for the “learning around the world” counter.
  • Google reCAPTCHA (through Firebase App Check) quietly checks that requests to our Firebase services come from this real website and not from automated scripts, to keep the counter and reminders free of spam. It runs in the background, never shows a puzzle, and works under the Google Privacy Policy and Terms.
  • GeoJS looks up an approximate country and town, only if you allowed the cookie notice (see the counter).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers reminders, only if you turn them on (see Reminders).
  • Google Fonts supplies the Tamil and English fonts. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) supplies a zip library, only on the Letter Recorder page.
  • Buy Me a Coffee: the “Buy me a coffee” link opens buymeacoffee.com, where you can choose to support the project. This website does not load any Buy Me a Coffee script and sends it nothing. If you decide to support, Buy Me a Coffee handles the payment under its own privacy policy, and may share with the creator the name and message you choose to leave.
  • YouTube, Instagram and our blog: the “Stay connected” boxes on guide pages, at the bottom of the home page, in the footer and in the Parent dashboard are plain links to our NikiBhavi YouTube channel, our Instagram page and blog.msdevbuild.com. No video player, feed, follow button or other script from those sites loads on our pages, and nothing is sent to them until you tap a link; then that site’s own privacy policy applies. These links are meant for parents and grown-ups: the YouTube and Instagram content is for adults, and Instagram requires users to be 13 or older. They never appear inside the children’s lessons, games, stories or certificates.
  • GitHub Issues is where you can report bugs and give feedback. Issues are public: your GitHub username and whatever you write can be seen by anyone, and are handled under GitHub's privacy statement. Please do not include personal details, especially about children. For private questions use the contact page.
  • Your browser's text-to-speech voice reads letters and words aloud. Some voices (for example Microsoft Edge “natural” voices or Google voices) are online services: your browser may send the text being spoken — Tamil letters and words from this site — to that provider. No personal information is part of that text.

Children's privacy

Learn Tamil is made for children. It never asks a child for personal details. The only personal information it keeps is the certificate record that a parent or guardian gives on the website, with their consent (see Certificate records). Anonymous usage statistics are collected only after a parent or teacher allows them, with advertising features off. There are no ads inside the lessons or games, no ad tracking, no chat and no links that ask for personal details; sponsor boxes and links to our YouTube, Instagram and blog appear only in grown-up areas (see Sponsor boxes). We recommend that young children use the site with a parent or teacher.
