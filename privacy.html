Privacy & data policy

Updated 9 November 2026 · By Suthahar Jegatheesan

🦜No tracking — promise!
In short: Learn Tamil does not collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. A few settings are saved only in your own browser.

Who runs this website

Learn Tamil (https://jssuthahar.github.io/learntamil/) is a free educational website made by Suthahar Jegatheesan under the MSDEVBUILD name. It is a personal, non-commercial project. Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page.

Information we collect

None. The site has no sign-up, no login, no forms and no comments. We do not ask for names, email addresses, ages, photos or any other personal details, and we do not run analytics or advertising scripts.

What is saved on your device

To make the site work, a few small values are saved in your browser's own storage. They never leave your device and we cannot see them.

WhatWhereWhy
Light or dark themelocalStorage lt-themeRemembers your colour choice
Game progress: tokens, badges, letters heard, words read, stories finishedlocalStorage lt-progressRemembers your child's rewards and progress
Name typed for a certificate, and certificate resultslocalStorage lt-cert-name, lt-progressPuts the name on the certificate and remembers which levels are passed. The certificate PDF is made inside your browser; the name is never sent anywhere.
Best game scores and sound on/offlocalStorage lt-best-*, lt-sfxShows the best score for each game; remembers if game sounds are muted
Closed voice messagesessionStorage lt-noticeKeeps the “no Tamil voice” message closed until the tab is closed
Your letter recordingsIndexedDB (only on record.html)Keeps recordings you make until you download or delete them

You can remove all of this at any time with Reset progress in the 🪙 My rewards panel, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Microphone

Only the optional Letter Recorder page uses the microphone, and only after you allow it and press Record. Recordings stay in your browser. They are never uploaded by this website; they leave your device only if you choose to download the zip file yourself.

Services provided by others

Like most websites, Learn Tamil relies on a few outside services. They may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser contacts them:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the Tamil and English fonts. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) supplies a zip library, only on the Letter Recorder page.
  • Buy Me a Coffee: the “Buy me a coffee” link opens buymeacoffee.com, where you can choose to support the project. This website does not load any Buy Me a Coffee script and sends it nothing. If you decide to support, Buy Me a Coffee handles the payment under its own privacy policy, and may share with the creator the name and message you choose to leave.
  • GitHub Issues is where you can report bugs and give feedback. Issues are public: your GitHub username and whatever you write can be seen by anyone, and are handled under GitHub's privacy statement. Please do not include personal details, especially about children. For private questions use the contact page.
  • Your browser's text-to-speech voice reads letters and words aloud. Some voices (for example Microsoft Edge “natural” voices or Google voices) are online services: your browser may send the text being spoken — Tamil letters and words from this site — to that provider. No personal information is part of that text.

Children's privacy

Learn Tamil is made for children and is designed so that it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no ads, no chat, no sharing features and no links that ask for personal details. We recommend that young children use the site with a parent or teacher.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Updated” date.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, please use the contact page.
