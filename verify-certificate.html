Check a certificate · சான்றிதழ் சரிபார்ப்பு

Updated 11 November 2026 · By Suthahar Jegatheesan

🦜Is it real? Let’s check! 🔎

Every certificate made on the Learn Tamil website has an ID printed at the bottom, such as LT1-AB12CD (letter levels) or TKL2-… and TK5-… (Thirukkural). Scan the certificate’s QR code, or type its ID below, to see the details it was made with.

What the check shows

  • The name, the level or Thirukkural chapter passed and the date the certificate was made: only what is printed on the certificate.
  • It never shows the parent’s email address or phone number.
  • Certificates can’t be listed or searched by name: you need the ID.

What you see for a real certificate

  • About this certificate: what the child achieved, what that level or Thirukkural chapter covers, the score, and how a certificate is earned in the app.
  • The certificate itself, exactly as it was printed, to download as a PDF or image.
  • Share buttons for WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Telegram, Instagram and email. The share message holds the level, the date and the link to this page, never the child’s name. Learn Tamil sends nothing: each button opens the app or site you choose.

How children earn a certificate

In the free Learn Tamil app, a child takes a level test: 10 multiple-choice questions (15 for the champion level), made fresh for every try from the app’s letters, words, sentences and Thirukkural. Passing needs 8 right (12 for the champion level), and each level opens only after the one before it is passed. A parent or guardian then gives the child’s name to make the certificate, and the record is saved so it can be checked here.

A certificate looks wrong, or you want a record corrected or deleted? Please use the contact page.
