The most useful Tamil phrases to start with are வணக்கம் (vanakkam, hello), நன்றி (nandri, thank you), ஆம் (aam, yes), இல்லை (illai, no) and உங்கள் பெயர் என்ன? (ungal peyar enna?, what is your name?). Below are easy everyday phrases with how to say them and what they mean. Use one new phrase at home every day.

💡

Polite Tamil uses “நீங்கள்” (neengal, you) and verbs ending in “-ங்கள்”, such as “சொல்லுங்கள்” (please say). Children learn these best by hearing grown-ups use them every day.

Learn the letters these phrases are made of on the Tamil alphabet page, and more words on family words and Tamil words for kids. Not sure how to say a sound? See the pronunciation guide.

❓Questions about Tamil phrases

How do you say hello in Tamil?

Hello in Tamil is “வணக்கம்” (vanakkam). It is said with the palms pressed together and works at any time of day, with anyone. “காலை வணக்கம்” (kaalai vanakkam) means good morning.

How do you say thank you in Tamil?

Thank you in Tamil is “நன்றி” (nandri). To say “thank you very much”, say “மிக்க நன்றி” (mikka nandri).

How do you say “What is your name?” in Tamil?

“What is your name?” in Tamil is “உங்கள் பெயர் என்ன?” (ungal peyar enna?). To answer, say “என் பெயர் …” (en peyar …), which means “My name is …”.

How do you say yes and no in Tamil?

Yes is “ஆம்” (aam) and no is “இல்லை” (illai). “சரி” (sari) means OK or all right.
