To install Learn Tamil, open learntamil.msdevbuild.com on your phone, tablet or computer and choose “Install app” (Android, Windows, Mac, Chromebook) or Share → “Add to Home Screen” (iPhone and iPad). It is free, needs no app store or account, and works offline after the first visit. Scan the QR code below to open it on another device.

⬇Install on this device

Your browser can install Learn Tamil right now.

✅ You are already using the installed app.

Follow the steps for your device below — your device is marked This device.

🎮 Open the learning app

📷Scan to install on another device

Point the phone or tablet camera at a code and tap the link that appears. Both codes are plain links to this website, with nothing added to track you.

Stick them on a classroom wall, a Tamil school notice board or the fridge.

📱Step by step for every device

🤖 Android phone or tablet

Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet

  1. Open learntamil.msdevbuild.com in Chrome (or scan the QR code).
  2. Tap ⬇ Install app at the top of the page — or the Install now button here.
  3. No button? Tap the ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. In Samsung Internet, tap ☰ → Add page to → Home screen.
  4. Open Learn Tamil from your home screen. It opens full screen, like any app.

🍎 iPhone or iPad

Safari

  1. Open learntamil.msdevbuild.com in Safari (or scan the QR code with the Camera app).
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ (at the bottom on iPhone, at the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Learn Tamil from your home screen.

🪟 Windows computer

Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome

  1. Open learntamil.msdevbuild.com in Edge or Chrome.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar — or the Install now button here.
  3. No icon? Open the browser menu (⋯ or ⋮) and look for Install Learn Tamil (in Edge under Apps; in Chrome under Cast, save and share).
  4. Learn Tamil appears in the Start menu and can be pinned to the taskbar.

💻 Mac

Safari, Chrome or Edge

  1. Safari (macOS Sonoma or later): open the site, then choose File → Add to Dock.
  2. Chrome or Edge: click the install icon in the address bar, or the Install now button here.
  3. Learn Tamil opens in its own window from the Dock or Launchpad.

🧑‍💻 Chromebook

Chrome

  1. Open learntamil.msdevbuild.com in Chrome.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar, or the Install now button here.
  3. Find Learn Tamil in the launcher with your other apps.
💡

The exact menu names can change a little between browser versions. If you can’t find the option, look in the browser menu for “Install” or “Add to Home Screen”.

💛Share Learn Tamil with family and friends

Know a family, teacher or Tamil school that would like it? Send them the link. Sharing opens the app you choose with a ready-made message, and nothing is sent until you tap.

❓Questions about the app

Is the Learn Tamil app free?

Yes. The Learn Tamil app is completely free, needs no account and has no in-app purchases. You install it straight from your web browser, so you do not need an app store.

How do I install Learn Tamil on iPhone or iPad?

Open learntamil.msdevbuild.com in Safari, tap the Share button, choose Add to Home Screen and tap Add. Learn Tamil then opens from the home screen like any other app.

How do I install Learn Tamil on Android?

Open learntamil.msdevbuild.com in Chrome and tap Install app at the top of the page, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. After the first visit, the letters, reading, stories, games, certificates and guides work without internet. Letter sounds offline need a Tamil voice stored on the device.

How do I remove the app?

On a phone or tablet, press and hold the Learn Tamil icon and choose Remove or Uninstall. On a computer, open the app, click the ⋯ menu in its title bar and choose Uninstall. Removing the app does not change the website.
