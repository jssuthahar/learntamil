Every chapter of Thirukkural is about one topic, so finding Kurals about kindness, friendship or learning means finding the right chapters. Here are 10 topics children and teachers ask about most, with the chapters for each and one Kural explained. Each chapter link opens all 10 of its Kurals with meanings.

💛 Kindness and gentle words📚 Learning and knowledge🤝 Friendship✅ Truth and honesty🙏 Gratitude, giving and helping🧘 Patience and self-control💪 Hard work and courage🏡 Family and home🌟 Good character and manners👑 Leadership and fairness

💛Kindness and gentle words

Love for everyone, compassion for every living thing, sweet words instead of harsh ones, and never hurting others — even those who hurt us.

  • Chapter 8: அன்புடைமை — The Possession of Love (Kurals 71–80) · 🔊 learn in the app
  • Chapter 25: அருளுடைமை — Compassion (Kurals 241–250) · 🔊 learn in the app
  • Chapter 10: இனியவை கூறல் — The Utterance of Pleasant Words (Kurals 91–100) · 🔊 learn in the app
  • Chapter 32: இன்னா செய்யாமை — Not doing Evil (Kurals 311–320) · 🔊 learn in the app

A Kural from this topic:

91

இன்சொலால் ஈரம் அளைஇப் படிறுஇலவாம்
செம்பொருள் கண்டார்வாய்ச் சொல்.

Meaning for children: Sweet words are words full of love and free of trickery, like the words wise people speak.

அன்பு கலந்த, ஏமாற்று இல்லாத, அறிவுடையவர்கள் பேசும் சொற்களே இனிய சொற்கள்.

G. U. Pope (1886): Pleasant words are words with all pervading love that burn; Words from his guileless mouth who can the very truth discern

A Kural from this topic:

391

கற்க கசடறக் கற்பவை கற்றபின்
நிற்க அதற்குத் தக.

Meaning for children: Learn what is worth learning, fully and well. Then live by what you have learned.

கற்க வேண்டியவற்றைக் குறை இல்லாமல் நன்றாகக் கற்க வேண்டும். கற்ற பிறகு அதன்படி நடக்க வேண்டும்.

G. U. Pope (1886): So learn that you may full and faultless learning gain, Then in obedience meet to lessons learnt remain

❓Questions

Which Thirukkural chapters are about kindness and gentle words?

Chapter 8, அன்புடைமை (The Possession of Love, Kurals 71–80); Chapter 25, அருளுடைமை (Compassion, Kurals 241–250); Chapter 10, இனியவை கூறல் (The Utterance of Pleasant Words, Kurals 91–100); Chapter 32, இன்னா செய்யாமை (Not doing Evil, Kurals 311–320). Love for everyone, compassion for every living thing, sweet words instead of harsh ones, and never hurting others — even those who hurt us.

Which Thirukkural chapters are about learning and knowledge?

Chapter 40, கல்வி (Learning, Kurals 391–400); Chapter 42, கேள்வி (Hearing, Kurals 411–420); Chapter 43, அறிவுடைமை (The Possession of Knowledge, Kurals 421–430); Chapter 41, கல்லாமை (Ignorance, Kurals 401–410). Learn well and live by what you learn; listen to wise people; knowledge is a wealth no one can take away.

Which Thirukkural chapters are about friendship?

Chapter 79, நட்பு (Friendship, Kurals 781–790); Chapter 80, நட்பாராய்தல் (Investigation in forming Friendships, Kurals 791–800); Chapter 82, தீ நட்பு (Evil Friendship, Kurals 811–820); Chapter 83, கூடா நட்பு (Unreal Friendship, Kurals 821–830). What true friendship is, how to choose friends carefully, and how to recognise false friends.

Which Thirukkural chapters are about truth and honesty?

Chapter 30, வாய்மை (Veracity, Kurals 291–300); Chapter 29, கள்ளாமை (The Absence of Fraud, Kurals 281–290). Speak the truth, even when it is hard, and never take what belongs to someone else.

Which Thirukkural chapters are about gratitude, giving and helping?

Chapter 11, செய்ந்நன்றியறிதல் (Gratitude, Kurals 101–110); Chapter 23, ஈகை (Giving, Kurals 221–230); Chapter 22, ஒப்புரவறிதல் (Duty to Society, Kurals 211–220); Chapter 9, விருந்தோம்பல் (Hospitality, Kurals 81–90). Remember every kindness you receive, give to those in need, help your community and welcome guests warmly.
