Learn Tamil is a free website and app that teaches all 247 Tamil letters with sound, reading, stories, rhymes, Thirukkural, 14 games, printable worksheets and certificates, for children aged 3–12 and beginners of any age. Here are answers to the questions parents, teachers and learners ask most. You can also ask 🦜 Kili, the help chat in the corner of every page.

❓Using Learn Tamil

How do I hear a letter?

Open 🔤 Learn (கற்றல்) and tap any letter tile. The big letter, its sound and a picture word appear at the top; press 🔊 to hear it again. Vowels play the letter and then their picture word. Open →

The sound is wrong or there is no sound

Sounds come from your device's Tamil text-to-speech voice, which can mispronounce single letters. To get a Tamil voice — Android: Settings → Text-to-speech → Google → install Tamil. Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → add Tamil. iPhone/Mac: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Tamil. Microsoft Edge has clearer Tamil voices. If you hear a mistake, please report it on GitHub Issues.

How do I install Learn Tamil as an app?

On Android, Chrome or Edge, tap the ⬇ Install app button at the top. On iPhone or iPad, open the site in Safari, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. The app opens full screen and has shortcuts to Letters, Reading, Stories, Games and Certificate. Steps for every device and a QR code to scan are on the install page. Open →

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit, letters, reading, stories, games, certificates and the guide pages work without internet. Sounds from the device voice work offline only if the voice is stored on the device.

How do I get a certificate?

Open 🎓 Certificate, choose one of the 10 levels and answer the questions (10 questions, pass with 8; the Champion level has 15, pass with 12). Type the name and download the certificate as a PDF. Each level unlocks the next, and every test asks new questions. Certificates are for encouragement, not an official qualification. Open →

What are tokens and badges?

Children earn 🪙 tokens for right answers, finished games, words read, stories and certificates, and unlock 🏅 badges such as “முதல் அடி” (first step). Tap 🪙 at the top to see them. They are saved only on this device.

How do I reset progress?

Tap 🪙 at the top to open My rewards, then press Reset progress. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Which games are there?

Fourteen games: க் + ஆ = ? (build a letter), Listen & pick, Spin wheel, Balloon pop, Word builder, Memory, Tamil quiz, Picture words, Topics, Spelling, Sentences, Trace (write letters with a finger), Colouring (colours in Tamil) and Little ones for ages 3–5. Every right answer gives points, a cheer and tokens; 3 in a row gives a bonus. Open →

Is my child’s data safe?

Yes. Learn Tamil collects no personal data: no accounts and no ad tracking. If a parent allows it, it counts anonymous visits and game use with Google Analytics for Firebase, with advertising features turned off. Progress and the certificate name are saved only in your own browser. See the Privacy & data policy. Open →

How can I report a mistake or give feedback?

Please open an issue on GitHub Issues. There are short forms for a wrong sound or Tamil mistake, a bug, or an idea. Issues are public, so please don’t include personal details.

How can I support Learn Tamil?

Learn Tamil is free, with no ads in the lessons or games. If it helps your family or classroom, you can buy me a coffee ☕. Businesses can also sponsor the parents’ guide pages: contact us to advertise. Thank you — உங்கள் ஆதரவுக்கு நன்றி!

Who made Learn Tamil?

Learn Tamil was made by Suthahar Jegatheesan (MSDEVBUILD) as a free, non-commercial project for children and beginners learning Tamil. More at blog.msdevbuild.com.

Where can I read Tamil stories?

Open 📖 Read → Stories & rhymes in the app to hear five short stories read aloud — tap any word to see its letters. You can also read them as text with English meaning on the Tamil stories page. Open →

Where can I download printable worksheets and charts?

Free printable PDFs — alphabet and picture-word charts, tracing worksheets for all 247 letters, word search, matching, missing-letter and joining worksheets and colouring pages — are on the printables page, or tap 🖨️ Printables in the menu. Print them on A4 or Letter paper. Open →

What word topics can my child learn?

In 📖 Read → Words choose a topic: numbers 1–10, colours, days of the week, parts of the body, family, fruits & food, animals, birds, vehicles, shapes, opposites and the 12 Tamil months. Each word shows its picture and letters and can be heard aloud. Open →

How can my child practise writing Tamil letters?

Tap ✍️ Trace under the big letter, or open Games → Trace. The letter is shown faintly; your child traces it with a finger, mouse or pen. When enough of the letter is covered it says the letter and gives a token. Printable tracing worksheets are on the printables page. Open →

What is Say it back?

Tap 🎤 Say it under the big letter or in Reading. Your child hears the right sound, records themselves saying it, then plays both to compare. The recording stays on the device only and is deleted when the window closes — it is never uploaded.

Are there Tamil rhymes and Aathichoodi?

Yes. Open Stories → 🎵 Rhymes for நிலா நிலா ஓடி வா, Bharathiyar's ஓடி விளையாடு பாப்பா and Avvaiyar's ஆத்திசூடி — one wise line for each vowel. Each word lights up as it is sung. A new Aathichoodi line of the day is on the home screen. Open →

Are there stories about Tamil festivals?

Yes. Open Stories → 🪔 Festivals for Pongal, Tamil New Year, Deepavali and Karthigai Deepam — a short read-aloud story and the special words for each. Open →

Where can my child learn Thirukkural?

Tap 📜 திருக்குறள் next to the letter tabs (or open thirukkural.html). All 1,330 Kurals are there by division and chapter. Each Kural can be heard (🔊, 🐢 slowly, or 🔁 say after me), and many have word meanings, a simple Tamil and English meaning, an animated story and a short quiz. There are six Thirukkural games, a Kural of the day, printable worksheets for every chapter and a certificate test for each chapter. Mark a Kural as learned to earn badges. Open →

Is there a mode for very young children?

Yes — 👶 Little ones (ages 3–5). Big pictures, no English text, sounds play by themselves and there is no wrong-answer buzzer. Open it from the home screen or Games → Little ones. Open →

Can brothers and sisters use the same device?

Yes. Tap the animal picture on the home screen card to switch child. Add up to 6 children in the 👪 Parent dashboard. Each child has their own tokens, badges, stickers and certificates, saved only on that device.

What is the Parent dashboard?

Tap 👪 Parents on the home screen and hold the button for 3 seconds. It shows your child’s age group and learning path (lessons done and what to try next), minutes learned each day, letters heard and traced, stories, certificates and the letters your child misses most. You can also add children and turn on a break reminder.

Which age is Learn Tamil for?

Children aged 3 to 12, and beginners of any age. On the home screen choose an age group: 👶 3–5 (pictures, sounds and colours in Little ones), 🧒 6–8 (letters, tracing and first words) or 🧑‍🎓 9–12 (reading, spelling, stories and certificates). Each age group has its own learning path of lessons, shown with ticks as they are done.

How is the app organised?

The menu has five parts: 🔤 Learn (all 247 letters), 📖 Read (words, stories, rhymes and festivals), 🎮 Play (14 games), 🖨️ Print (free worksheets and charts) and 🎓 Certificate (10 level tests).

Can I make the text bigger or change the sound?

Yes. Tap ⚙️ at the top. You can choose Large or Extra large text, turn on high contrast, use less movement, turn game sounds on or off, and change the voice speed and volume. The settings are saved only on this device.

What is the daily gift?

After your child learns the first thing each day, the 🎁 on the home screen card opens for bonus tokens. The longer the day streak, the bigger the gift (up to 10 tokens). Missing a day never takes anything away.

What is the sticker book?

Every 10 tokens 🪙 unlock a new picture sticker with its Tamil name. Tap 📒 Stickers on the home screen to see the book and tap a sticker to hear its name.

How does reading practice work?

Open 📖 Read → Words. Read the word yourself, tap 🐢 to hear it letter by letter, then show the picture to check. There are three levels: 2, 3 and 4+ letter words. Open →

📬Still need help?

Report a wrong sound, a Tamil mistake or a bug on GitHub Issues (issues are public, so please don’t include personal details). For private questions, use the contact page.
