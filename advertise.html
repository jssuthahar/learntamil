When you enquire, we can share recent visit numbers, counted anonymously and only in total.

🛡️Where ads will never appear

To keep Learn Tamil safe for children, sponsor ads are never shown in the learning app’s lessons, games, stories, rhymes, quizzes or certificates, and the Learn Tamil Android app shows no ads at all. This is a promise we make to parents, and it is part of what makes the guide pages a trusted place for your brand.

📜Advertising policy

These rules apply to every sponsor on Learn Tamil. Price, placement, dates and other terms are agreed with each business in writing before an ad is published.

✅ Advertisement approval

  • All advertisements are subject to our review and approval before they are published.
  • We may reject, pause or remove any advertisement that does not follow this policy.
  • Advertisers must give accurate business and promotional information.

📝 Advertising content

  • Advertisers are responsible for the legality and accuracy of their advertising, and must own or have a licence for every image, logo and word they send.
  • We only accept child-safe, family-friendly advertising. We do not accept misleading, fraudulent, illegal or inappropriate advertising, or advertising for adult content, gambling, alcohol, tobacco or vaping, weapons, or dating services.
  • Ads must not ask children to buy anything or to give personal details.

🔒 User privacy

  • We never share our visitors’ personal information, email addresses, phone numbers or other private data with advertisers.
  • Advertisers have no access to any user data. Learn Tamil has no accounts and collects no personal information.
  • Ads are plain images or text with a link: no advertising scripts, tracking pixels or cookies, and no targeting or personalisation.
  • Business details sent to us in an enquiry are used only to handle that enquiry and the advertisement, and are kept private.

⚖️ Advertisement disclaimer

  • Every paid placement is clearly labelled “Sponsored”, and its link is marked as sponsored for search engines.
  • We do not guarantee sales, leads, traffic or any other business result.
  • Advertisers are responsible for their own products, services, offers and customer transactions.

🤝 Terms and conditions

  • Pricing, placement, duration, renewal, cancellation and refunds are agreed directly with the business before publication. There is no online booking or payment on this site.
  • We may update our advertising formats and this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.

❓Questions from advertisers

How do I advertise on Learn Tamil?

Contact us through the contact page at blog.msdevbuild.com/contact with your business name, contact details and the ad format you are interested in. We reply to discuss placement, dates and price. There is no online booking or payment on the site.

Where do sponsor ads appear on Learn Tamil?

Only in grown-up areas: the parents’ and teachers’ guide pages and the parents’ area of the home page. Ads never appear inside the children’s lessons, games, stories or certificates, and the Learn Tamil Android app shows no ads at all.

Does Learn Tamil use Google Ads or track visitors for advertisers?

No. Learn Tamil does not use Google AdSense or any ad network. Sponsor ads are plain images or text with a link. They load no advertising script, set no cookies and are not personalised, and no visitor data is shared with advertisers.

What kinds of businesses can advertise?

Child-safe, family-friendly businesses, such as bookshops, Tamil classes, schools, tutors, children’s clothing, cultural events and family services. Every ad is reviewed and must be approved before it goes live.

How much does advertising cost?

Prices depend on the format, the pages and how long the ad runs. Contact us and we will share the current options and price for your campaign. Price, dates, renewal and cancellation are agreed with you before anything is published.
